"At a basic level, computers have a few important parts: 
the CPU (processor) executes instructions and performs calculations; 
RAM temporarily holds information programs are actively using; 
storage such as an SSD keeps files and programs even when the power is off; 
and the motherboard connects the major components. 
A GPU specializes in graphics and highly parallel calculations,
while input/output devices—such as a keyboard, mouse, monitor, microphone, 
and speakers—let the computer interact with you."
